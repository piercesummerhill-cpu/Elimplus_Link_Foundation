Our focus

Initiative framework

Skills for school, life, and the future.

Building future skills

Code Tanzania Clubs, Digital Media Labs, and Critical Thinking Debate Clubs are designed to build computational thinking, communication, technical skills, and evidence-based reasoning.

Fostering leadership & innovation

The ELF Student Innovation Challenge, Peer Leadership Programs, and Model United Nations simulations are designed to help learners collaborate, lead, research, and solve local problems.

Promoting health & well-being

School Gardening & Nutrition, Mindful Classrooms, Guidance & Counseling Corners, and wellness workshops are designed to support healthy, focused learning environments.
