About ElimuPlus-Link Foundation

Education that connects learners to opportunity.

ELIMUPLUS-LINK FOUNDATION (ELF) is a Tanzanian-based non-profit organization founded by educators, community leaders, and reformers.

Our vision

A Tanzania where every learner is equipped with the knowledge, skills, and character to drive positive change and excel in the global community.

Who we are

Helping education prepare learners for the future.

ELF works to bridge the gap between the education system and the skills learners need to succeed in a global marketplace. We support educators and promote equitable learning environments so more young people can access quality, relevant, and inclusive education.

Our mission

To transform education in Tanzania by developing and supporting highly effective teachers and leaders, creating innovative and inclusive learning models, and advocating for a safe, equitable, and technology-enhanced educational environment for every child.

Our commitment

We put learners first and work with schools, communities, families, partners, and existing systems to pursue sustainable educational change.

Our values

Guided by integrity and learner-centered change.

Transparency Integrity Child-centricity Inclusion Passion for change Respect for Tanzanian law

ELF believes sustainable progress in education is built through responsible collaboration, thoughtful innovation, and respect for the communities and learners we serve.
