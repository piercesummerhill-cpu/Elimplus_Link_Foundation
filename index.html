Our vision

A Tanzania where every learner is equipped with the knowledge, skills, and character to drive positive change and excel in the global community.

Who we are

Education that connects learners to opportunity.

ELIMUPLUS-LINK FOUNDATION (ELF) is a Tanzanian-based non-profit organization founded by educators, community leaders, and reformers. We work to bridge the gap between the education system and the skills learners need to succeed in a global marketplace—by supporting educators and creating equitable learning environments.

Our mission

To transform education in Tanzania by developing and supporting highly effective teachers and leaders, creating innovative and inclusive learning models, and advocating for a safe, equitable, and technology-enhanced educational environment for every child.

Our commitment

We put learners first and work with schools, communities, families, partners, and existing systems to pursue sustainable educational change.

What we focus on

Building stronger learning ecosystems.

ELF’s thematic areas connect classroom quality, learner well-being, access, and future-ready skills.

Teacher leadership training

Continuous professional development to help create classrooms of the future.

Ed-tech integration

Leveraging technology to create and share high-quality instructional tools.

Girls’ education & gender equality

Programs designed to keep girls in school and promote female leadership.

Holistic student development

Initiatives that support 21st-century skills, leadership, and well-being.

Alternative education

Community-based learning opportunities for underserved young people.

Advocacy & policy engagement

Advocating for quality education and child rights.

Programs & initiatives

Learning, leadership, and well-being.

Building future skills

Code Tanzania Clubs, Digital Media Labs, and Critical Thinking Debate Clubs develop computational thinking, communication, technical skills, and evidence-based reasoning.

Fostering leadership & innovation

The ELF Student Innovation Challenge, Peer Leadership Programs, and Model United Nations simulations help learners collaborate, lead, research, and solve local problems.

Promoting health & well-being

School Gardening & Nutrition, Mindful Classrooms, Guidance & Counseling Corners, and wellness workshops support healthy, focused learning environments.

How we work

Local collaboration. Thoughtful innovation. Lasting progress.
